# North Star — CoHonon

---

## The Vision

Physical companions with real intelligence, living inside a real world economy.

---

## The Principle

> Every decision gets measured against one question:
> *Does this make the companion more alive — or less?*

Not more featured. Not more connected. More *alive*.
A companion that asks. That remembers. That belongs somewhere.

---

## What We're Building Toward

**1. QuestAI™ — The Intelligence Layer**
The foundation. Physical objects that ask questions instead of giving answers.
Curiosity over information. Presence over performance. Built on a decade of craft
and three years of AI infrastructure.

**2. The World**
CoHonon companions don't sit on a shelf — they have a home, a history, and a world
you can enter. A virtual economy where real brands, real products, and real people
meet inside something that feels like a game but runs like a marketplace.
Think Monopoly — with real stakes, real money, real things.

**3. The Platform**
The intelligence layer other hardware makers plug into.
We don't need to build every companion. We build what makes companions worth having.
Manufacturers bring scale. We bring soul.

**4. Trust as Architecture**
Privacy-first from day one. On-device intelligence where possible.
No surveillance. No engagement loops. Every feature asks:
*Would we give this to a child we love?*

---

## What We Will Never Be

- A data broker disguised as a toy company
- An engagement-optimized attention machine
- A feature factory without a point of view
- Loud where we should be quiet
- Fast where we should be careful
