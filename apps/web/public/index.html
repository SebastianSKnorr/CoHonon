The Problem

We built machines that think.
We forgot to teach them to care.

The physical world runs on trust. When a device enters someone's home, their workshop, their child's room — it carries implicit weight. It is present. It is watched. It is felt.

Most AI was designed to be useful. Almost none of it was designed to be safe in the places that matter most. The gap between those two things is where CoHonon operates.

The Platform

QuestAI™

A framework for AI you can actually trust — because you can see exactly where it meets the world.

One Drop One line of machine learning.
A Glass A model you can audit and understand.
An Ocean A large language model — vast, powerful, and opaque.

You cannot see into the middle of the ocean. That is not a flaw — it is physics. But we know exactly where the ocean meets the shore. That thin layer — where intention becomes output, where model meets person — is the most consequential surface in AI.

QuestAI™ is built around that surface. It governs the layer where AI behaviour intersects with human reality. Not to constrain what AI can do — but to ensure that what it does is legible, auditable, and aligned with the values of whoever built it.

2026

Canal Creatures is a line of physical AI companions designed for children — creatures that live in the real world, built on QuestAI™. They listen, they respond, they remember. And they are safe by design, not by accident.

They are our proof of concept and our first commercial deployment.

Roots

Built on real work in Northern Europe.

Nordic AI and Com2.ai were founded to close the gap between AI research and physical deployment. Not to publish papers — to ship systems. CoHonon is the next chapter of that work: focused, applied, and accountable.

Founder

Sebastian Knørr

Before AI, there was wood. Sebastian trained as a møbelsnekker — a Norwegian furniture craftsman — where precision and material honesty were non-negotiable. That discipline followed him into technology.

He co-founded Nordic AI and Com2.ai, and developed the QuestAI™ framework independently — a philosophy of AI that questions rather than answers, that creates presence through curiosity rather than information. He speaks internationally on responsible physical AI deployment and the psychological frontier of human–machine interaction.

Canal Creatures was co-founded with designer Coco — Qiongke Li. Sebastian comes from the Norwegian mountains. That matters.
